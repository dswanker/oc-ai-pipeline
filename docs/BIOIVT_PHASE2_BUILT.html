3. DIN

Every DIN question in both studies is now text, at most 30 characters, no other pattern.

StudyQuestions
DetroitSHORTFORM.DIN, OUTCOME.DIN
PrecisionDM.DIN, DM.FOLLOW_DIN, DMONC.DIN

Longest seen: 18 in the Detroit data (81,272 participants; lengths 8 to 18, most 12 to 16) and 15 in the Carlsbad data (002-6504-660002; digits and hyphens). 30 leaves headroom because only the ten most common Detroit lengths were available. A first cap of 15 would have blocked about 13% of Detroit values.

How it fails safe. The form looks up the answer from the subject's Short Form (any event). If found: the original question hides and a read-only line shows the value. If not found for any reason: the original question shows and works exactly as it does today. Nothing can become un-enterable. The read-only line shows stored codes as they are (for example a race code), not the long label. 'Other race, specify' is left alone: it already hides whenever Race is hidden.

6. Not in this build (and why)

  • Merging the duplicated lab, therapy and diagnosis blocks into shared forms. Rebuilds repeating groups across several forms in both studies, with no extra visible lookup for the demonstration. Next iteration.
  • Age 18 or over calculation. The eligibility form may be completed before the Short Form exists, so a read-only calculation could leave it unanswerable.
  • 'Other race, specify' autofill (an existing rule owns it; it still hides with Race).
  • An 'Other, specify' box for the six new unit lookups (the units list has an Other row but no text box behind it on those six).
  • Vaccines, anatomic locations, tumour staging, surgical procedures: not coded; they need other vocabularies or your design decisions.
  • Precision: only the DIN rule and the medication lookup change.

7. What could go wrong

  1. OC4 acceptance is untested: the new forms, lookups of this size (up to 9,090 rows) and the cross-form lookups. I recommend the free scratch-study upload test before the paid run.
  2. The validator cannot evaluate cross-form lookups, including those in forms already published (Date of Visit, Vital Signs, Veterinary). With those rows set aside, every form validates clean.
  3. Calculations not executed. Syntax is valid; real behaviour is confirmed only in OC4.
  4. Licences. SNOMED CT and LOINC content in a client demonstration needs the licences confirmed before real use.
  5. Run on a fresh study (V4) rather than re-running the published V3 boards, and run once.
